# Competitor Intel Brief Examples

## Example 1: SaaS Competitor With Hidden Pricing

### Signal
- Hero says "for teams of all sizes"
- No pricing page
- Case studies all show 500+ employee logos

### Correct Interpretation
- Implied ICP is broad
- Actual ICP is enterprise or upper mid-market
- Hidden pricing supports a high-ACV, sales-led motion

## Example 2: Content-Heavy Competitor

### Signal
- Blog updates weekly
- LinkedIn posts daily
- Reviews complain about onboarding time and support lag

### Good Output
- Do not say "they own content" and stop
- Translate the review pain into an angle:
  - faster implementation
  - clearer onboarding
  - lower switching anxiety

## Example 3: Attack Brief

### Vulnerability
- Competitor sells breadth but not speed to value

### Good Separation Angle
- "Built for teams who need implementation this quarter, not a six-week rollout."
